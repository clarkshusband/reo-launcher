# Reo Launcher Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 29, 2026

This policy explains what information Reo Launcher, made by drako ("we", "our" or "us"), handles and why. We keep this as small as possible: most of what the Launcher stores stays on your computer.

## 1. Stored on your computer

- **Account sign-in tokens** from Microsoft, so you stay signed in. These are stored locally and only sent to Microsoft and Mojang.
- **Settings, instances, mods, worlds and logs.** These stay on your computer unless you choose to share them.

## 2. Sent over the internet

- **Microsoft and Mojang:** sign-in, your Minecraft profile and skin, and game files.
- **Polyfrost:** the Launcher downloads version lists and other metadata from Polyfrost's servers, checks their service status, and uses their backend for some online features. These requests include your IP address and may include your Minecraft profile when a feature needs it. Polyfrost's handling of that data is covered by the [Polyfrost Privacy Policy](https://polyfrost.org/legal/privacy).
- **Modrinth and CurseForge:** searches and mod downloads you make.
- **Java vendors** (such as Adoptium, Azul, Amazon and Microsoft): Java runtime downloads.
- **Crash reports (Sentry):** if the Launcher crashes, it can send a crash report with technical details such as your operating system, Launcher version and the error. Crash reports do not include your password or sign-in tokens.
- **mclo.gs:** only when you press the button to upload and share a log.
- **Discord:** if Discord is running, the Launcher shows what you are playing through Rich Presence on your own machine.

## 3. What we don't do

We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps or websites.

## 4. Declining

If you decline the Terms of Service and this Privacy Policy, the Launcher stops contacting Polyfrost services and stops sending crash reports. You can still sign in and play. You can change your choice later under **Settings > Launcher**.

## 5. Children

The Launcher is not directed at children under 13. If you are under the age of digital consent where you live, use it with a parent's or guardian's permission.

## 6. Your rights

You can delete everything the Launcher stores locally by signing out and uninstalling it. To ask about data we may hold, such as crash reports, contact us on [Discord](https://discord.gg/reomc).

## 7. Changes

We may update this policy. When we do, the Launcher shows the new version and asks you to accept it.
